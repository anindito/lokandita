# Lokandita Brand Family — Variation 1

Seven brands: Lokandita, LokaKode, LokaUjian, LokaKelas, LokaSiber, LokaRetas, LokaData.

This ZIP contains individual logo assets extracted from the approved silver-embossed-ring concept, rather than whole presentation-board screenshots. All PNGs and WebPs have **transparent outer backgrounds** so they can be placed on websites in their corresponding theme. The circular silver rings and interior faces are retained.

## Asset categories (each of the seven brands)

- `01-logo-only`: dark and light square 512 × 512 px PNG + WebP. Use for app tile, avatar and compact logo placement.
- `02-logo-name-below`: dark and light square 1024 × 1024 px PNG + WebP, wordmark below the emblem.
- `03-logo-name-right`: dark and light horizontal 1600 × 520 px PNG + WebP, wordmark on the right.
- `04-favicon`: dark and light multiresolution ICO containing 16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px, plus individual 16, 32, 48, 180 and 192 px PNG icons.

Dark assets: light wordmarks with a dark badge interior; best on charcoal/dark backgrounds. Light assets: dark wordmarks with a white badge interior; best on white/light backgrounds.

## How to use in a website

Prefer `.webp` for regular logo content when supported. PNG offers broad compatibility, transparency and design-editing convenience. The `.ico` or 32 px PNG goes in the site's favicon tags. For example:

```html
<link rel="icon" href="/assets/LokaKode_light_favicon_32.png" sizes="32x32" media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)">
<link rel="icon" href="/assets/LokaKode_dark_favicon_32.png" sizes="32x32" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">
```

Use the original preferred background colors approximately `#0e1520` (dark) and `#f4f7fb` (light).

## Important

These are *raster* exports from the selected image concept, not hand-traced, infinitely scalable vector/SVG designs. The wordmarks have been typeset consistently in a bold geometric sans-serif; silver ring illustrations match the approved concept. For later print or brand-registration needs, a separately designed vector master would be preferable.
